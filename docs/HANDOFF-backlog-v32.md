## Result

- New three-night runs use adventure v5: a fixed batch of three pages. Completing one page leaves an empty slot until the batch is finished. Returning from shops, objects or healing does not destroy their page. Only its top-right X closes that optional page. Stock, receipts, purchases, healing counters and fusion use persist through reentry and saves. A used chest cannot award another card. Mandatory story, ghosts and bosses cannot be deleted.
- Global skip-three is removed for v5. One actual intersection page per night can defer unfinished ghosts, while retaining services/story; quota and boss gates still require all 27 route fights. Walking animation only accompanies a new batch/intersection; returning to an entered board is immediate. Legacy adventure v3/v4 saves keep their original lifecycle; start a new run to use the new page rules.
- Defeated route ghosts do not repeat across the three-night run. All 27 identities are selected from existing illustrated ghosts; normal/elite encounter rank is independent of their roster tier. No new ghost artwork is required. Cold-save structural tests cover all four classes and all five difficulties, including the 31st secret-boss fight.
- Each class has two first-night build kits using existing cards plus a conditional blessing. Guard/refund, repeated strikes/strength, enemy status/extra damage, longer-lived minions, healing/draw, block/wheel, summon/refund and poison/weakness are different plans. Existing equipment supports these plans. Triggers are broad and bounded once per turn; upfront HP costs and a safe gold alternative are shown before confirming. Early level-up pools also allow removal, upgrade or blessing. This does not prove that both directions are equally fun or equally strong.
- Six named ghosts receive readable opening rules using existing thorns, regeneration, dodge or strength. Existing owner decks/signature moves remain for other ghosts. Rule details open through the status icons, without next-action intent or a lore button on the battle HUD.
- Opening draw 2, energy 2, turn draw 3, retained-hand cap 6 for new runs. Draw, cap and energy are separate fields; draw upgrades also grow opening draw/cap. The QA พระประธาน adds one extra opening card in test APKs only. Do not use it for balance evidence.
- Battle uses full-body existing ghost art, a subdued existing scene, compact enemy/player HUDs, separate horizontal status/minion/blessing rows and six exposed card corners in a shallow fan. Long hands remain browsable. Completed combo notices have a reserved gap outside the enemy HUD; only completion notifies, multiple completions queue, and pending-count/deadline details live on the icon/book.
- Enemy reveal has a static owner anchor; only local translation/scale/opacity animate. It responds to viewport changes and retains a readable dwell under reduced motion. Runtime verification is still required; do not invent a diagnosed device-specific root cause.
- Shop card purchase confirmation sits outside the product scroller. Equipment, removal, upgrade and fusion confirmations keep actions outside their detail scrollers. Actual rewards have frozen receipts with card art/name/cost/upgrade/effect, blessing art/name/effect, equipment, loss and HP/gold changes. UI display never grants or rerolls rewards.

The initial Android export failed because local react-native-screens was 4.29.0 while the unchanged lockfile specifies 4.16.0; reinstalling the locked dependencies fixed it without a dependency/source patch. The tests cover fixed batches, optional-page X, shop reentry/stock/cold saves, single-use chests, actual receipts, old v4 saves, separate draw/energy, builds, combo deadlines and geometry at 360x640 and 393x852. Campaign tests additionally assert 27 distinct route identities in all 20 class/difficulty combinations and restore state/RNG on every screen.

Local Playwright execution was blocked because its browser download returned a truncated ZIP. No local mobile/native success is claimed. Existing no-capture CI checks were updated for v5, fixed confirmation visibility, six-card geometry and the static enemy-card face. Android API36 verification uses the QA card to check flow, unique ghosts and cold saves; it cannot establish gameplay balance or fun.

Normal-card trials (no QA card): draw2 and draw3 used identical 80-run samples; wins improved from 17/80 to 40/80, with no stalls. Final draw3 pilot: 240 runs across 4 classes × 5 difficulties × 2 policies × 6 seeds, 105 wins and zero stalls. Public-board difficulty1 wins: warrior6/6, shaman6/6, nun5/6, medium4/6. Mean player turns were 83.7/82.2/131.5/51.7 respectively. The automatic policy selects the first build option; these are small samples and do not prove balance for both kits. Medium at higher difficulties and nun pacing need real-player feedback. Sixty minutes is a target for an entire three-night run, not measured human playtime.
