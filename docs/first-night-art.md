# First-night art

Built-in imagegen was used for two project assets. All referenced final files are in the repository; no runtime generation or API credentials needed.

- `assets/scence/episode-village.jpg`: opaque 768×1152 JPEG, downscaled from the generated portrait. Rustic deserted Thai village path, hut, banana grove and plain temple roof; heavy uneven black brush ink, faded ochre/olive/brick/bone palette, coarse halftone. Open middle for sprites, low-contrast lower area for UI. No characters, text, logos, ornate fantasy or cinematic realism.
- `assets/monsters/phi-krasue-pulp.png`: 512×512 RGBA PNG, true alpha (range 0–255), downscaled from generated square. A floating woman's head, messy black hair, cheeky alarming eyes and toothy grin, simplified red ribbon-like entrails, complete isolated silhouette. Same rough black ink and faded four-color pulp print; no scenery, halo, realistic anatomy, lettering or fantasy accessories.

The original `phi-krasue.png` remains archived in-place but is no longer the wired Krasue image. Existing Pop, Tanee, dancer, Pong Kang and four class sprites were inspected/reused to keep scope small. The chapter/event share the village location image. Rest/upgrade offer illustrations reuse the existing pulp rest scene. Story panels and combat/encounter frames use code-rendered ink borders, not ornate decorative frame images.

5–10 minutes is the intended human reading/decision/combat duration; automated simulations do not measure that. Art should be reviewed on the APK for small-screen readability before expanding the roster.
